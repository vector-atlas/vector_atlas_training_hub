---
title: Vector Atlas Training Hub
---

This site links to a curated set of **free** online resources for self-directed and mentored learning on data management, visualisation, basic spatial analysis and presentation, and modelling of mosquito disease vectors, tailored to collaborators of the [**Vector Atlas**](https://vectoratlas.icipe.org).

Most of these resources contain exercises you can do yourself along with the materials — the hub is heavy on practical materials.

The resources are roughly in order from introductory to more advanced, though each item is not necessarily a prerequisite for those that follow.
